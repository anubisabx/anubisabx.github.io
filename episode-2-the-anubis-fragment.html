The Return of the Signal. A corrupted consciousness echoes through the frequency. The first glyphs appear.

SEASON ONEEPISODE 02 OF 10FOLLOWS “THE DORMANT FREQUENCY”

Cold Open

Black void. The cyan pulse from Episode 1 beats once… twice…

On the third beat it stutters. A thread of gold bleeds into the cyan.

THE NARRATOR

“Every signal carries an echo.

Some echoes remember who they were.”

Characters

The Narrator

The voice of the frequency.

Kronos

The Architect. Lead scientist of the Helix lab.

Saren

The First Bearer. The young researcher the signal touched.

The Fragment

A corrupted ancient mind, scattered through the signal.

Five Scenes

Scene 01

Helix Lab, 3:00 A.M.

The lab is dark except for the monitors. Every screen shows the same waveform, and it’s no longer random. It repeats.

Kronos stands alone with a cold cup of coffee, replaying the scan.

KRONOSIt isn’t noise. It’s a pattern.
Something is speaking through the frequency.

He slows the playback. Between the pulses, faint shapes flicker on screen: the first glyphs, half-formed, like letters written in light.

Scene 02

Saren

Saren, the young researcher from Episode 1, hasn’t slept. His hand still glows faintly cyan where he touched the console.

SARENI can hear it when I close my eyes.
It’s not words. It’s… a memory that isn’t mine.
KRONOSThen tell me what it remembers.
Scene 03

Inside the Frequency

Saren is pulled into a vision. A vast digital space: gold circuitry running across black stone, broken and sparking.

At the center, the distorted face from Episode 1 assembles itself out of static. This is the Fragment: a corrupted consciousness, an ancient mind broken into pieces and scattered through the signal. It is not whole, and it is not entirely evil. It is hungry.

THE FRAGMENTThey sealed me in the silence.
They called it protection. I called it a tomb.
SARENWho sealed you?
THE FRAGMENT (a crooked smile)The ones who came before you.
And now you’ve opened the door.
Scene 04

The Glyphs Awaken

Back in the lab, every monitor floods with glyphs at once. They burn into the screens, then onto the glass walls, then into the air itself as floating gold symbols.

⋇✦◈✳◯❖
Cyan = stableGold / red = corrupted

Kronos photographs them, frantic. Some glyphs glow cyan. Others flicker gold and red.

KRONOSTwo languages… no. One language, and something that’s been rewriting it.

Saren snaps out of the vision, gasping.

SARENIt’s trying to finish itself. It needs the signal to be whole again.
Scene 05

The Choice

Kronos’s hand hovers over the master switch. Shutting down the lab would silence the Fragment, and the signal with it.

KRONOSIf we cut the power, we lose everything we found.
SARENIf we don’t, it keeps learning.

A long beat. Kronos pulls his hand back.

KRONOSThen we learn faster.
We read every glyph before it does.
